About Teflex

Engineered for the toughest sealing environments

Precision · Performance · Perfection

Teflex Engineering & Co. specialises in the design and manufacture of high-performance PTFE sealing and engineering components. Every part is CNC-machined in-house to exacting tolerances, giving procurement teams and engineers a dependable single source for standard and custom parts.

We combine deep material knowledge of PTFE and filled-PTFE grades with disciplined quality control — so components perform reliably across chemical, thermal and mechanical extremes.
